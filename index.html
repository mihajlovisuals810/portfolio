Questions

FAQ

How long does a typical edit take?+
Most short-form edits (under 60 seconds) are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving the footage. Larger batches or more complex projects may take 2–3 days.
What do I need to send you?+
Raw footage, any reference videos you like the style of, and a brief description of the goal. The more context you give, the better the first draft will be.
How many revisions are included?+
Revisions are included until you're satisfied. I don't cut corners on quality — if something isn't right, we fix it.
What platforms do you optimise for?+
TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts primarily. Each platform has its own pacing, caption style and aspect ratio — I tailor every edit to fit where it's going.
Do you work with ongoing clients?+
Yes — most of my work is with recurring clients on a weekly or monthly basis. If you're posting consistently, reach out and we can set up a package that works for both of us.
